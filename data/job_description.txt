JOB TITLE: Junior Generative AI Engineer

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are looking for an entry-level engineer interested in building LLM-powered applications and retrieval-augmented generation systems.

REQUIREMENTS
- Proficiency in Python.
- Understanding of embeddings and vector databases.
- Familiarity with LangChain or similar LLM frameworks.
- Understanding of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- Ability to work with REST APIs and backend services.
- Basic understanding of machine learning model evaluation.
- Good problem-solving and communication skills.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Build document ingestion and retrieval pipelines.
- Generate embeddings and retrieve relevant document chunks.
- Integrate LLMs into Python applications.
- Develop and test backend APIs.
- Evaluate response quality and investigate retrieval failures.

PREFERRED SKILLS
- Experience with ChromaDB.
- Familiarity with prompt engineering.
- Understanding of hallucinations and grounding.
- Experience building academic or personal GenAI projects.
